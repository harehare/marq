# Plan first

## research

Investigate only: read the code, the notes and earlier sessions to learn what the task needs. Do not change any files.

End your reply with the findings the next phase needs: the relevant files and how they work, constraints, risks and open questions.

## plan

Plan only: using what the research found, write a step-by-step plan with the files to change and how, in order, and how to check the result. Do not change any files.

End your reply with the plan.

## implement

Finish the task, following the plan. Where the plan turns out to be wrong, say what you did instead and why.
